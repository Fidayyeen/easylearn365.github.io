My Professional Journey

My professional journey has been shaped by curiosity, hands-on learning, and a steady move toward modern cloud and AI-enabled technologies.

I started by building a strong foundation in IT operations, workplace technologies, endpoint management, and user support. That foundation helped me understand how real business environments work, how users depend on technology every day, and why reliability, security, and clear documentation matter.

Over time, my focus expanded into Microsoft 365, identity and access management, cloud administration, and security. Working across these areas helped me see how modern IT is connected: users, devices, identities, data, policies, automation, and cloud services all need to work together.

As my skills continue to grow, I am intentionally moving toward AI Platform Engineering. For me, that means combining cloud infrastructure, identity and security, automation, DevOps practices, Power Platform, and AI services to build practical, governed, and scalable solutions.

Today, I feel ready to help small and medium-sized businesses harness the power of technology, automation, cloud platforms, and AI-enabled solutions to improve productivity, solve real business challenges, and move closer to their goals.

EasyLearn365 is part of that journey. It gives me a place to document what I learn, reflect on how I build, and share practical lessons while also showing how I can support businesses and be part of their success story.
