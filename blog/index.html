Blog

The EasyLearn365 blog will document learning publicly and share technical knowledge through practical, structured posts.

Blog Purpose

This section is for students, beginners, IT professionals, and anyone who prefers practical, documented examples over theory alone.

The blog will focus on learning by building, configuring, testing, troubleshooting, and documenting real scenarios.

Main Topics

  • Microsoft 365 administration and modern workplace concepts
  • Intune and endpoint management
  • Entra ID and identity management
  • Azure administration, services, and architecture
  • AI enablement, Copilot, and Azure AI services
  • Power Platform and productivity automation
  • Git, GitHub, and documentation-driven development
  • Linux and IT fundamentals
  • The EasyLearn365 website build journey

Post Types

  • Learning notes
  • Hands-on labs
  • Troubleshooting notes
  • Project documentation
  • Certification notes
  • Reflection posts

Suggested Post Structure

  1. Title
  2. What I was trying to learn or solve
  3. Tools or technologies used
  4. Steps followed
  5. What worked
  6. Problems or mistakes encountered
  7. What I learned
  8. Next improvement or follow-up

Publishing Approach

Blog content will be added gradually. The first version will stay simple and content-focused. Advanced styling, search, filters, categories, and automation can be added later after the core website content is complete.
