# Purpose

The Services section will explain the areas where Sakib can provide technical help, guidance, or future professional services.

The goal is to present value clearly while keeping the section honest, practical, and aligned with current skills and learning direction.

---

# Audience

This section should be useful for:

- Small businesses looking for Microsoft 365 or cloud guidance
- Professionals or beginners looking for technical mentoring
- Clients who need help understanding modern workplace, identity, endpoint, automation, or AI-related solutions
- Recruiters or collaborators who want to understand Sakib's practical focus areas

---

# Service Direction

The Services page should not feel like a sales-heavy page at the beginning.

It should clearly explain areas of knowledge, possible support, and professional direction. As experience, projects, and portfolio examples grow, this page can become more specific.

The first version should focus on clarity, credibility, and practical value.

---

# Potential Service Areas

## Microsoft 365 Setup and Administration

Guidance or support related to Microsoft 365 tenant setup, user management, collaboration tools, Teams, Exchange, SharePoint, and modern workplace configuration.

## Intune and Endpoint Management

Support related to device management, endpoint policies, compliance, configuration profiles, application deployment, and endpoint security basics.

## Identity and Access Management

Guidance related to Entra ID, users, groups, roles, authentication, access control, conditional access concepts, and secure identity practices.

## Cloud and Azure Guidance

Support related to Azure fundamentals, Azure administration, cloud service planning, PaaS concepts, and practical cloud learning or implementation scenarios.

## AI Enablement and Productivity Solutions

Guidance related to AI productivity, Copilot adoption, Azure AI concepts, workflow improvement, and AI-enabled solutions for small and medium-sized businesses.

## Power Platform and Automation

Support related to Power Automate, Power Apps, workflow automation, approvals, forms, and productivity-focused business process improvements.

## Technical Mentoring and Training

Practical guidance for beginners, students, or professionals learning Microsoft 365, Azure, Git, GitHub, cloud fundamentals, documentation, or modern IT workflows.

# Suggested Services Page Structure

The future Services page can follow this structure:

1. Page title
2. Short introduction
3. Who the services are for
4. Main service areas
5. How Sakib can help
6. Current focus and limitations
7. Contact or next step

---

# Tone and Positioning

The Services page should sound:

- Professional
- Honest
- Practical
- Clear
- Helpful
- Not exaggerated

The page should avoid claiming deep expertise in areas that are still being actively learned. It can say that the site and service direction are evolving alongside ongoing learning, projects, and practical experience.

---

# Important Rules

- Do not overpromise services.
- Do not list services that cannot be supported with knowledge or future portfolio examples.
- Do not add pricing yet.
- Do not add booking systems yet.
- Do not add advanced forms yet.
- Keep the first version simple and content-focused.
