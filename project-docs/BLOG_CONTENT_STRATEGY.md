# Purpose

The Blog section will document learning publicly and share technical knowledge through practical, structured posts.

The goal is to make the blog useful for:

- Students and beginners learning IT concepts
- IT professionals exploring Microsoft 365, Azure, identity, automation, and AI
- Anyone who prefers practical, documented examples over theory alone
- Sakib's own learning record and professional growth

---

# Blog Direction

The blog should focus on learning by building, configuring, testing, troubleshooting, and documenting real scenarios.

Posts should be written in a clear, practical style. They do not need to be perfect or overly advanced at the beginning. The priority is to document what was learned, what was tried, what worked, what failed, and what improved.

# Recommended Blog Post Types

## Learning Notes

Short posts that explain a concept learned recently.

## Hands-On Labs

Step-by-step posts showing how something was configured, tested, or deployed.

## Troubleshooting Notes

Posts documenting an issue, the investigation process, the fix, and lessons learned.

## Project Documentation

Posts that explain a practical project, including problem statement, tools used, implementation steps, and outcomes.

## Certification Notes

Posts summarizing useful concepts from certification learning paths and how they apply in real environments.

## Reflection Posts

Short posts about learning progress, project decisions, mistakes, improvements, and future goals.

# Suggested Post Structure

Each technical blog post can follow a simple structure:

1. Title
2. What I was trying to learn or solve
3. Tools or technologies used
4. Steps followed
5. What worked
6. Problems or mistakes encountered
7. What I learned
8. Next improvement or follow-up

This structure can evolve later, but it gives the blog a simple starting format.

# Publishing Approach

Blog content should be added gradually.

At the beginning, the blog can stay simple and content-focused. Advanced styling, search, filters, categories, and automation can be added later after the core website content is complete.

---

# Important Rules

- Do not build the blog platform yet.
- Do not create blog folders yet unless implementation is the active step.
- Do not add advanced styling yet.
- Do not overcomplicate categories.
- Keep the first posts simple and practical.
